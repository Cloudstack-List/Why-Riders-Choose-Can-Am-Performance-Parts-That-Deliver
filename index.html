OEM vs Aftermarket

Understanding the difference between OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) and aftermarket Can-Am Collection accessories is essential for selecting parts that match your performance goals.

OEM Parts:
OEM parts are manufactured to the exact specifications of the original Can-Am components. They offer guaranteed compatibility and maintain the manufacturer’s warranty standards. While reliable, OEM parts sometimes lack the performance edge riders seek for extreme conditions.

Aftermarket Performance Parts:
Aftermarket Can-Am parts are designed to exceed OEM specifications. Manufacturers often focus on improving durability, horsepower, torque, and handling. Many aftermarket parts are engineered to withstand harsher conditions and provide a more aggressive performance profile than stock components.

Key Consideration:

  • OEM parts maintain reliability with minimal risk.

  • Aftermarket parts boost performance, often at competitive pricing.

  • Ensure aftermarket parts are compatible with your specific Can-Am model to avoid installation or warranty issues.

For riders looking to push the limits, investing in high-quality aftermarket Can-Am UTV accessories can deliver measurable gains in both power and longevity.

Engine & Exhaust Upgrades

Installing upgraded exhaust systems, high-flow air filters, and performance intakes improves engine efficiency, horsepower, and throttle response. Riders experience smoother acceleration, particularly when climbing steep hills, towing, or carrying heavy loads. Quality aftermarket performance parts are designed to reduce engine strain, minimize heat buildup, and extend the lifespan of your vehicle’s powertrain. These components are precision-engineered to optimize fuel efficiency while delivering noticeable gains in speed and responsiveness.

Suspension & Handling

Suspension is critical for both comfort and control on challenging trails. Upgraded shocks, A-arms, and sway bars absorb impacts more effectively, reducing stress on the frame and other components. Reinforced A-arms and heavy-duty sway bars enhance stability when cornering at speed or carrying additional weight. When paired with premium UTV accessories such as reinforced skid plates and chassis guards, these upgrades protect your vehicle while improving ride smoothness and handling precision.

Tires & Wheels

High-performance tires and durable wheels are essential for traction and stability across diverse terrain. Aftermarket tires are engineered for rocks, mud, sand, and mixed trail conditions, while reinforced wheels provide strength under heavy loads and aggressive maneuvers. Choosing the right combination of tires and wheels ensures your Can-Am UTV maintains grip, cornering control, and safety in all environments. It offers a selection of rugged tires and wheels specifically designed to complement other performance upgrades.

Brake Systems

Upgraded brake pads, rotors, and calipers improve stopping power and responsiveness, which is critical when navigating steep descents, sharp turns, or unpredictable trail surfaces. Performance brakes minimize fade during extended rides and provide consistent control even under high-speed or heavily loaded conditions. Investing in high-quality brake components ensures riders can fully exploit the power of their upgraded Can-Am while maintaining safety and confidence.

When to Upgrade

Deciding when to invest in performance parts depends on riding habits, terrain, and goals.

Signs it’s Time to Upgrade:

  • Frequent Rough-Terrain Riding: If your UTV spends most of its time on rocky or uneven trails, upgrading the suspension and tires improves both comfort and vehicle longevity.

  • Towing or Heavy Loads: Engine and brake upgrades ensure your UTV performs reliably under strain.

  • Desire for Higher Performance: Riders seeking faster acceleration, sharper handling, and improved overall efficiency benefit from aftermarket upgrades.

  • OEM Parts Wearing Out: Replacing worn-out OEM parts with high-performance aftermarket alternatives can improve durability and reduce maintenance.

Strategic upgrades maximize performance without unnecessary modifications. Combining engine, suspension, and accessory improvements often yields the most noticeable results.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Are aftermarket Can-Am performance parts compatible with all Can-Am UTV models?
Compatibility varies by model and year. It is crucial to verify specifications before purchase. Trusted suppliers, like Can Am Collections, provide detailed fitment guides.

2. Will aftermarket upgrades void my warranty?
Some upgrades may impact warranty coverage if installation alters OEM components. Installing parts through certified technicians and consulting manufacturer guidelines reduces risk.

3. Do performance upgrades require professional installation?
While some parts can be installed at home, complex upgrades such as engine components or suspension kits often require professional installation for optimal performance and safety.

4. Can I combine multiple upgrades at once?
Yes, layering compatible upgrades such as exhaust, suspension, and tires can produce cumulative benefits in power, handling, and durability. Planning upgrades strategically ensures maximum results.

5. Are Can-Am UTV accessories worth the investment?
High-quality UTV accessories enhance performance, safety, and ride enjoyment, making them a worthwhile investment for serious riders who demand reliability and capability.
